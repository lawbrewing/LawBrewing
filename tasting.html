It's Was All a Dreamsicle

SOURED MILKSHAKED JUICY IPA
IBU: 33
ABV: 5.6%

You thought you knew IPAs? Nah, son. This ain't your daddy's bitter brew. This is "It Was All a Dreamsicle," and trust, it’s about to change the game.

We took the old-school juicy vibe and flipped the script. We soured it up—dropping a little tartness just to let you know this brew got hustle—then we milkshaked the remix. We’re talking a heavy dose of lactose and vanilla for a body that’s thicker than a bassline and smoother than a velvet tracksuit.

Then comes the drop: Dolcita hops. A straight-up flavor powerhouse delivering sticky-sweet candied orange. It’s a tart, creamy, citrus masterpiece. It smells like money. It tastes like success. Juicy. Sour. Milkshaked. Legendary.

Stolem

BALTIC PORTER
IBU: 32
ABV: 6.2%

In the twilight dominion of pine and salt spray, where black waves crash against the stones, a Kashubian Princess raises her glass.

Inside the vessel lies a Baltic Porter with a soul as deep as the night—a dark mirror to the Stolem who guards her realm. His towering stride shakes the earth, ensuring no corner of the kingdom goes unseen; yet, in the glass, his power is tamed.

Here, thunderous strength dissolves into a velvet embrace. Whispers of espresso and burnt caramel rise like sea mist, echoing the giant’s heavy step. The beer’s smooth warmth floods the senses like a rising tide, a potent reminder to all who partake: The Princess, the Stolem, and the spirit of Kashubia stand eternal.

Gutless Rusty

BOURBON PUMPKIN VANILLA PORTER
IBU: 51
ABV: 6.2%

Gutless Rusty is a bold, brooding vanilla pumpkin spiced porter aged on bourbon-soaked oak, brewed in defiance of convention and in honor of the legendary XFL coach Rusty Tillman—the man who stood his ground when Jesse “The Body” Ventura tried to play puppetmaster.

This beer is a rich, smoldering rebellion in a glass: layers of roasted malt and velvety pumpkin glide over a backbone of warm spice and smooth vanilla, while hints of charred oak and bourbon remind you that even when you’re called “gutless,” standing firm still leaves a hell of a legacy. Sip it like Rusty coached—without compromise.
