THE TAPROOM

A Beer Flight Card Game




The Rules

OBJECTIVE

Win 3 shifts (rounds) to win the match. To win a shift, have the highest scoring flight when Last Call ends.

THE TURN

  1. 1. The Serve: Start with 4 cards. Do ONE:
    • Play a Brew (2-10): Place in Flight (Max 4).
    • Play a Patron (J, Q, K, A): Discard for effect.
    • Waste: Discard 1 card from hand.
  2. 2. The Pour: Draw cards until you have 4 again.
